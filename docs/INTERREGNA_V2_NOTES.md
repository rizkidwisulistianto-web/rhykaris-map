# Interregna mosaic v2 — redrawn borders and sixteen new placeholder polities

Political-layer change of 2 Oct 2026, built on branch `feat/interregna-mosaic`. It redraws every border inside the old Interregna footprint and adds sixteen unnamed polities in four types (kingdom, free city, tribal land, micro-polity), with enclaves, one nested enclave, one exclave and a few pockets that are *not* enclosed. Epistemic labels follow the project's four levels: **Kanon** (locked), **Turunan** (derived from canon), **Inferensi AI** (proposed, provisional), **Terbuka** (deliberately unlocked).

The reason is cartographic, and the request said so plainly: the old Interregna looked like a honeycomb — a ring of near-identical blobs — whereas the corridor is huge and, in canon, is a mosaic of very different things. Nothing here is canon; every new polity is **Inferensi AI**, unnamed (*tak bernama*) and labelled *ilustratif* in its card.

## 2. Why the old drawing looked wrong

The old block grew nine polities with identical parameters (same bias, same range cap 750, same zone radius 4.0°, the same cost field) from seeds on a loose lattice. The result is what identical rules give: areas within 3.9× of each other, similar rounded shapes, every polity the same kind of thing, no polity inside another. Real borders have a heavy-tailed size distribution, elongated and lobed shapes that follow valleys and foothills, and a few pockets that history left behind. The new set has:

| | before | after |
|---|---|---|
| Polities inside the footprint (without frozen Cassivalla, Nundina) | 9, all kingdoms | **25**: 13 kingdoms (Aventalia and Tarvenna included), 6 free cities, 3 tribal lands, 3 micro-polities |
| Largest ÷ smallest area | 3.9× | **182×** (ir_2 8,184 px ≈ 1.23 M km² · ir_19 45 px ≈ 6,800 km²); the 13 kingdoms alone span 12× |
| Ring vertices | 184 | 709 |
| Enclaves (fully inside one other polity) | 0 | **7**, one of them nested two deep (ir_23 ⊂ ir_13 ⊂ ir_2) |
| Pockets that are *not* enclosed | 0 | **4** (a city at the northern tip, a three-border city, two micro-polities) |
| Exclaves (detached piece) | 0 | **1** (a piece of ir_14 inside ir_6) |

## 3. Canon basis, and what stays open

- **Kanon** (Atlas — Interregna, Powers — Interregna): "belasan-lebih kerajaan kecil bekas vassal … masing-masing berdaulat"; the order Cassivalla (fallen, east) → Tarvenna (on the campaign route) → … → Aventalia (still thinks it has time, west); the Florian front is the border of Provincia Cassivallae / Silva Nullius against Tarvenna; Nundina is a free city.
- **Turunan**: the mosaic typology — kerajaan vassal of the Era Serpihan, kota merdeka, wilayah adat, mikro-polity — gives the four types `K`, `C`, `A`, `M`. With Cassivalla the map shows 14 kingdoms, which is "belasan".
- **Inferensi AI**: how many, where, how big, which are enclaves, the colours. All of it.
- **Terbuka — deliberately not decided here**: which polity is next on Florian's list; the *Satvan Perbatasan* enclave (knob #3); the eastern border; the final count and the names of the kingdoms. The new polities do not point at any of these (the validator keeps Tarvenna touching Cassivalla and the order west → east, and nothing more).

## 5. How the borders are drawn

`src/interregna.py` is deterministic (seed 1647) and repo-relative; it needs `work/terrain_4096.npz` (step 1 of the pipeline).

1. **Footprint.** The legacy partition of `politics.py` is reproduced to recover *F*; Cassivalla and Nundina are frozen; the remainder (50,053 px) is repartitioned.
2. **Cost.** The legacy movement cost × a slope penalty × fine noise. Only the *great* rivers are barriers, so a kingdom does not leap one, while city-states creep along river valleys (cost × 0.6 there).
3. **Growth.** One multi-source Dijkstra per polity with its own low-frequency "corridor" noise (fingers and bays), an anisotropy along a chosen axis (valleys, foothill ribbons) and a penalty zone so no finger crosses half the map.
4. **Areas.** Per-polity weights are fitted so each polity reaches its target area, in two stages — the second one measures after warp and filtering, so the targets survive post-processing (largest error of the base partition 1.9 %). Pockets then take their pixels from whatever they sit in: an enclave from its host (budgeted in advance), a free pocket from its neighbours (spread evenly in the budget), so the final areas drift a little more where free pockets bite — worst cases `ir_7` −17 % and `ir_10` −10 % against their targets, inside the tolerances of §6.
5. **Edge character.** A three-scale domain warp (big bays, medium bends, fine wrinkles), a mode filter, a size-dependent opening that removes slivers, and a clean-up pass that gives orphaned pixels to their majority neighbour.
6. **Pockets.** Enclaves are cut from inside their host, leaving a 2 px collar so they are genuinely enclosed; the area is exact (an order statistic on the cost distance, not a bisection). Free pockets are cut around a seed that the table puts on a border junction (the validator checks the outcome, §6). The exclave is cut from another host and assigned to its owner.
7. **Rings.** Each polity's boundary is traced and simplified (709 vertices in total, 9–84 per polity). The renderers draw every ring as its own polygon without holes, so an enclave is a polygon over its host and relies on the drawing order.
8. **Colours.** A graph colouring (DSATUR with backtracking) in CIE-Lab over a muted 16-colour palette: touching polities ≥ 26 ΔE apart and an enclave ≥ 40 ΔE from its host — the suggestion fails rather than relax this. The Aventalia, Tarvenna, Hesperia, Foedera, Cassivalla, Nundina and Kloaka colours are fixed and taken into account. The validator's floor is 20 ΔE.

## 6. Guard rails — what the validator enforces

`python src/interregna.py` prints the report; **`--write` refuses to write if any check fails.**

- every pixel of the area to repartition has exactly one label, and the union with the frozen pixels is exactly *F*;
- each polity is one connected component (plus its exclaves); its area is within 22 % of its target for kingdoms, 15 % for the two canon anchors, 40 % for the rest (a warning only);
- the canon anchors (Aventalia, Tarvenna, the Cassivalla marker) fall inside their polygons; Tarvenna touches Cassivalla by ≥ 25 px; the centroids run Aventalia < Tarvenna < Cassivalla from west to east; Aventalia does not touch Cassivalla;
- **an enclave touches nothing but its host and the pockets inside it; an exclave touches nothing but its host; a pocket declared free touches ≥ 2 neighbours (another polity or the edge of the footprint) by ≥ 6 px each**, so the sentence "berdiri bebas (bukan enklaf)" in a card can never disagree with the geometry. (Two pockets failed this check in an early draft; it is now enforced.);
- every ring is closed and does not cross itself; ring-versus-label IoU ≥ 0.90 (kingdoms, tribal lands) or 0.75 (cities, micro-polities) — a warning;
- no two touching polities are closer than 20 ΔE in colour;
- the rings cover *F* with IoU ≥ 0.985.

One warning remains and is accepted: `ir_13` has ring/label IoU 0.89, because its filled ring also covers the micro-polity nested inside it.

## 7. Re-running it

```
python src/terrain.py                 # only if work/terrain_4096.npz is missing (≈ 190 MB, bit-identical)
python src/interregna.py              # dry run: report only (~20 s)
python src/interregna.py --preview ir.png --colors     # polygon preview; colour suggestion
python src/interregna.py --write      # rewrites the Interregna block of data/politics.json, nothing else
```

`build_data.py` still carries the archive's absolute paths (see the README), so mirror that folder, build, and copy back:

```
mkdir -p /home/claude/rhykaris_map && cd /home/claude/rhykaris_map
ln -s <repo>/src/*.py .  &&  ln -s <repo>/work/terrain_4096.npz .  &&  cp <repo>/data/politics.json .
python build_data.py && cp data.json <repo>/data/data.json
python <repo>/scripts/build_index.py
```

`--write` is idempotent (a second run yields the same bytes), and `build_index.py` reproduces the committed `index.html` byte for byte from an unchanged tree.

**To change the mosaic**, edit only `POLITIES` in `src/interregna_table.py`: `seed`, `target` (px), `axis` and `aniso` (direction and elongation), `r_km` (a pocket), `host` (an enclave of that polity), `exclaves`. Keep the rows in drawing order (host before pockets) and the ids stable. Run the dry run, fix what the validator reports, run `--colors` if the neighbours changed, then `--write` and rebuild.

## 8. Limits, and one discrepancy that was left alone

- **Resolution.** A pixel is 12.7 km, so the smallest city-states are polygons of 9–12 vertices; at world zoom they are dots, which is the point. A border is not claimed to be more accurate than the grid.
- **Rings have no holes.** An enclave is drawn on top of its host (the host's ring is filled where it surrounds a pocket). Anything that measures area from rings alone would double-count a pocket; the `px` in `politics.json` is exclusive.
- **Hover and click** pick the topmost polygon, so a pocket wins over its host; the dual-disk map's hover test (`tests/disk.mjs`) walks every territory and expects exactly that.
- **Cards.** Each new card carries *Inferensi AI*, its type, and — computed from the table — which polity it is inside or which it holds. Tribal lands (type `A`) get a dashed outline, as the viewer already does for Kloaka and Vasundha.
- **Pre-existing discrepancy, not touched.** Rebuilding `data.json` from the committed sources gives `elev` 3055 for Ktonia and 1234 for Pylora, whereas the committed `data.json` carries 2940 and 1140. This change restores the committed values so that its diff is only the Interregna layer; whichever is right belongs in a separate change.
- **Not done.** No new places or labels; no naming; no change to the physical map or to the 3D relief; the canon assets in `assets/` (including the SVG preview) are untouched.
- **Documentation screenshots not retaken.** The pictures in `docs/images/` that include the corridor (at least `01-world-map`, `03-territories` and `09-dual-disk`) still show the old drawing, small at world zoom. They are made with production fonts that the build sandbox cannot download, so they are left for a session that can. *(Retaken in v1.7.0 — see `POLITICS_V3_NOTES.md` §9.)*
